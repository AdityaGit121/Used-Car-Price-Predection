05 / MATHEMATICAL FORMULATION

Prediction Calculation & Decision Engine

Translating raw regression outputs into practical negotiation strategies for buyers and sellers.

📐

1. Fair Value & Error Band

The model infers fair market value Ŷ. The error band is defined as [max(0, Ŷ - MAE), Ŷ + MAE], providing an honest confidence band reflecting dataset dispersion.

⚖️

2. Asking Price Divergence

Calculates variance: Δ = Asking Price - Ŷ.
Percentage difference: |Δ| / Ŷ × 100%.
Divergences within 5% are classified as Fair Market Price.

🤝

3. Dual Guidance Strategy

If Above (>5%): Buyer is cautioned to negotiate closer to Ŷ. Seller is advised to lower price.
If Below (<-5%): Buyer alert for high deal value. Seller notified of potential underpricing.

06 / MLOPS & RETRAINING

Admin Management & Production Deployment Gate

Ensures continuous model improvement without risking regressions in production accuracy.

📂

Batch Data Ingestion & Hygiene

Admins upload batch CSVs at /admin. The system enforces required schema columns (Brand, Model, Year, Age, kmDriven, Transmission, Owner, FuelType, AskPrice), parses mixed currency and mileage strings, and removes duplicate listings.

🔒

The Automated Safety Gate

When retraining is triggered, candidate models are stored in models/registry/v{N}. Activation endpoint /admin/deploy/{v} strictly evaluates: Candidate RMSE < Production RMSE. If the candidate does not beat the champion, it is rejected!
